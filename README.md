# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Analysis Using Python

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores customer shopping behavior using Python. The goal is to analyze purchasing patterns, identify popular products, understand payment preferences, and discover seasonal shopping trends.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze customer shopping data.
* Identify the most popular product categories and items.
* Explore payment methods and seasonal purchasing patterns.
* Understand customer subscription behavior.
* Examine customer reviews, discounts, and purchasing habits.
* Present findings through charts and business insights.

## 🧰 Tools and Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Google Colab

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **3,900 records and 18 original columns**, covering customer demographics, purchased items, purchase amounts, product categories, payment methods, seasons, review ratings, and subscription status.

## 🔎 Analysis Performed

* Data inspection and quality checks.
* Descriptive statistical analysis.
* Purchase amount analysis by product category.
* Popular product analysis.
* Payment method analysis.
* Customer analysis by gender and age group.
* Seasonal shopping analysis.
* Review rating analysis.
* Discount and subscription analysis.
* Dashboard visualization.
* Business insights generation.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* **Top Category:** Clothing recorded the highest total purchase amount at $104,264.
* **Most Frequently Recorded Item:** Blouse appeared in 171 records.
* **Most Common Payment Method:** PayPal appeared in 677 records.
* **Top Season:** Fall recorded the highest total purchase amount at $60,018.
* **Subscription Status:** "No" was the most common status, appearing in 2,847 records.
* **Average Review Rating:** 3.75 out of 5.
